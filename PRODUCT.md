# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Bid and proposal specialists at small-to-mid defense suppliers. Every morning they triage the new DLA solicitations posted to DIBBS, at a desk on a large monitor, scanning hundreds of rows to shortlist the handful worth quoting. They are power users: they know NSNs, FSCs, CAGE codes and set-aside types, they work fast, and they return to the tool daily.

## Product Purpose
A fast, focused browser for DIBBS Requests for Quotation. It pulls the daily RFQ files, collapses them into one row per solicitation, and lets a vendor search, filter and sort down to the opportunities that match their product classes, eligibility and capacity, then jump straight to the original record on DIBBS to quote. Success: a specialist gets from "new drop" to "shortlist" in minutes, not an hour of paging through DIBBS.

## Brand Personality
Precise, calm, fast. The interface is quiet chrome around the data, like Linear or a restrained Bloomberg terminal: identifiers read like identifiers, deadlines are unmistakable, and nothing competes with the table. Tone of copy is terse and expert; no marketing voice.

## Anti-references
- DIBBS itself: 2005 government portal, tiny Verdana, blue bordered tables, cramped postback grids, 50 rows per page with numbered paging.
- Generic SaaS dashboards: KPI hero tiles, charts for their own sake, card grids. The table is the product.

## Design Principles
1. **The table is the product.** Density and scannability beat decoration; every pixel of chrome must earn its place.
2. **Deadlines first.** Time left to quote is the most decision-relevant fact and must be readable at a glance.
3. **Speak the domain.** Use DIBBS vocabulary (NSN, FSC, set-aside, CAGE) and formats (SPE1C1-26-Q-0309, 8315-00-075-0244) exactly, so experts trust it.
4. **Honest data.** Show what the source actually says (e.g. "Posted", not a guessed issue date) and where it comes from.
5. **Always one click from DIBBS.** Every row leads back to the original record.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Sensible defaults: AA contrast for text, full keyboard operation with visible focus, reduced-motion respected, and status never conveyed by color alone.
